Annual Sports Meet 2025

Date: 1 Oct 2025   |   Venue: Sports Ground

About the Event

The Annual Sports Meet 2025 is a celebration of athletic excellence, teamwork, and the spirit of sportsmanship. Students from all departments will compete in various track and field events, team games, and individual competitions, showcasing their skills and passion for sports.

The event aims to promote fitness, discipline, and healthy competition among students, while also recognizing outstanding talent with awards and accolades.

Event Highlights

  • 100m, 200m & 400m Races
  • Relay Races (4x100m)
  • Long Jump & High Jump
  • Shot Put & Javelin Throw
  • Cricket & Football Finals
  • March Past & Torch Relay

Schedule

8:00 AM – 8:45 AM Inauguration Ceremony & Torch Relay
9:00 AM – 12:00 PM Track Events: 100m, 200m, 400m races & Relay
12:15 PM – 2:00 PM Field Events: Long Jump, High Jump, Shot Put, Javelin
2:15 PM – 5:30 PM Team Games: Cricket & Football Finals
6:00 PM – 7:00 PM Closing Ceremony & Prize Distribution

Key Details

Date: 1 Oct 2025 Venue: Sports Ground Theme: Fitness, Teamwork & Excellence

Participation

All departments are invited to participate. Events are open to both boys and girls. Winners will receive medals, trophies, and certificates of achievement.
