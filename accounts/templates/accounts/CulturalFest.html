Cultural Fest 2025

Date: 20 Sept 2025   |   Venue: Main Auditorium

About the Event

The annual Cultural Fest is one of the most awaited events of the college calendar. It brings together vibrant performances, artistic showcases, and a celebration of diversity. Students from all departments unite to express their creativity through music, dance, drama, fashion, and more.

This year, the theme of the fest is "Unity in Diversity". Expect a colorful blend of traditional and modern acts, interactive competitions, and opportunities to experience different cultures in a single day.

Event Highlights

  • Inaugural Dance Performance
  • Inter-college Singing Competition
  • Theatrical Skits and Mime Acts
  • Fashion Show: Fusion of Cultures
  • Art and Photography Exhibition
  • Closing Ceremony with Celebrity Performance

Schedule

9:00 AM – 10:00 AM Opening Ceremony & Lamp Lighting
10:30 AM – 12:00 PM Dance & Music Competitions
12:30 PM – 1:30 PM Drama & Theatrical Performances
2:00 PM – 3:30 PM Fashion Show & Cultural Fusion Acts
4:00 PM – 6:00 PM Closing Ceremony & Guest Performance
