Tech Symposium 2025

Date: 25 Sept 2025   |   Venue: Seminar Hall

About the Event

The Tech Symposium 2025 is a premier gathering for technology enthusiasts, innovators, and budding engineers. It offers a platform for showcasing cutting-edge research, presenting projects, and exploring the latest advancements in technology.

With expert talks, hands-on workshops, and competitive events, the symposium encourages knowledge sharing and inspires participants to think beyond boundaries. This year’s theme is “Innovating for a Smarter Future.”

Event Highlights

  • Keynote by Industry Experts
  • Paper Presentation Competition
  • Hackathon: 12-hour coding challenge
  • Robotics Demonstrations
  • Panel Discussion: Future of AI & ML
  • Project Exhibition by Students

Schedule

12:15 PM – 1:15 PM Panel Discussion: AI & ML Trends
2:00 PM – 6:00 PM Hackathon & Robotics Demo
